(72)
Chapter: To one who intends to give food or drink
(72)
الدعاء إذا أراد الطعام أو الشراب
Hisn al-Muslim 183
Allāhumma aṭ`im man aṭ`amanī wasqi man saqānī. O Allah, feed the one who has fed me, and give drink to the one who has given me drink. Reference: Muslim 3/126.
اللّهُـمَّ أَطْعِمْ مَن أَطْعَمَني، وَاسْقِ مَن سقاني
Reference  : Hisn al-Muslim 183
