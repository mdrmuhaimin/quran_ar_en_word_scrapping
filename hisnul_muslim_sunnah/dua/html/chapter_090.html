(90)
Chapter: To one who has offered you some of his wealth
(90)
الدعاء لمن عرض عليك ماله
Hisn al-Muslim 201
Bārakallāhu laka fī ahlika wa mālik. May Allah bless you in your family and your property. Reference: Al-Bukhari, cf. Al-Asqalani, Fathul-BAri 4/88.
بارَكَ اللهُ لَكَ في أَهْلِكَ وَمالِك
Reference  : Hisn al-Muslim 201
