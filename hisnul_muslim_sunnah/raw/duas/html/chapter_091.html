(91)
Chapter: To the debtor when his debt is settled
(91)
الدعاء لمن أقرض عند القضاء
Hisn al-Muslim 202
Bārakallāhu laka fī ahlika wa mālik, innamā jazā'us-salafi ‘l-ḥamdu wa‘l-adā'. May Allah bless you in your family and your wealth, surely the reward for a loan is praise and returning (what was borrowed). Reference: An-Nasa'i, 'Amalul-Yawm wal-Laylahp. 300, Ibn Majah 2/809. See also Al-Albani, Sahih Ibn Majah2/55.
بارَكَ اللهُ لَكَ في أَهْلِكَ وَمالِك، إِنَّما جَـزاءُ السَّلَفِ الْحَمْدُ والأَداء
Reference : Hisn al-Muslim 202
