Chapter: To someone wearing a new garment
(4)
الدعاء لمن لبس ثوباً جديداً
Hisn al-Muslim 7
Tublī wa yukhliful-lāhu ta`ālā. May Allah replace it when it is worn out. Reference: Abu Dawud 4/41. See also Al-Albani Sahih Abu Dawud 2/760.
تُبْـلي وَيُـخْلِفُ اللهُ تَعَالى
Reference : Hisn al-Muslim 7

Hisn al-Muslim 8
Ilbas jadīdan, wa `ish ḥamīdan, wa mut shahīdan. Put on new clothes, live a praise-worthy life and die as a martyr. Reference: Ibn Majah 2/1178, Al-Baghawi 12/41. See also Al-Albani, Sahih Ibn Majah 2/275
اِلبَـس جَديـداً وَعِـشْ حَمـيداً وَمُـتْ شهيداً
