Chapter: When slaughtering or offering a sacrifice
(127)
ما يقول عند الذبح أو النحر
Hisn al-Muslim 246
Bismillāh wallāhu Akbar [Allāhumma minka wa lak] Allāhumma taqabbal minnī. With the Name of Allah, Allah is the Most Great! [O Allah, from You and to You.] O Allah, accept it from me. Reference: Muslim 3/ 1557, Al-Bayhaqi 9/287.
بِسْمِ اللَّهِ وَاللَّهُ أَكْبَرُ [اللَّهُمَّ مِنْكَ وَلَكَ] اللَّهُمَّ تَقَبَّلْ مِنِّي
