Chapter: Of the guest for the host
(71)
دعاء الضيف لصاحب الطعام
Hisn al-Muslim 182
Allāhumma bārik lahum fī mā razaqtahum, wagh’fir lahum warḥamhum. O Allah, bless them in what You have provided for them, and forgive them and have mercy on them. Reference: Muslim 3/1615.
اللّهُـمَّ بارِكْ لَهُمْ فيما رَزَقْـتَهُم، وَاغْفِـرْ لَهُـمْ وَارْحَمْهُمْ
Reference : Hisn al-Muslim 182
