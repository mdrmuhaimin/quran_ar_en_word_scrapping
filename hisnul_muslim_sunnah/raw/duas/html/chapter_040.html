Chapter: For one afflicted with doubt in his faith
(40)
دعاء من أصابه شك في الإيمان
Hisn al-Muslim 133
A`ūdhu billāh (Say:
) I seek refuge in Allah. (Then you should desist from doing what you are in doubt about.) Reference: Al-Bukhari, cf. Al-Asqalani, Fathul-Bari 6/336, Muslim 1/120.
يَسْتَعِيذُ بِاللَّهِ.
يَنْتَهِي عَمَّا شك فِيهِ.

Hisn al-Muslim 135
