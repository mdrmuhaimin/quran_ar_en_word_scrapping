Chapter: Stopping or lodging somewhere in travel and otherwise

الدعاء إذا نزل منزلا في سفر أو غيره
Hisn al-Muslim 216
A`ūdhu bikalimāti ‘llāhit-tāmmāti min sharri mā khalaq. I seek refuge in the Perfect Words of Allah from the evil of what He has created. Reference: Muslim 4/2080.
