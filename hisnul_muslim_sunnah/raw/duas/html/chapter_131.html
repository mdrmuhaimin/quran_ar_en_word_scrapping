Chapter: How the Prophet ﷺ made tasbeeh
(131)
كيف كان النبي ﷺ يسبح ؟
Hisn al-Muslim 266
Abdullah bin 'Amr (RA) said:
"I saw the Prophet (ﷺ) counting the glorification of his Lord on his right hand." Reference: Abu Dawud with a different wording 2/81, and At-Tirmidhi 5/521. See also Al-Albani, Sahihul-Jami'As-Saghir 4/271 (no. 4865).
عَنْ عَبْدِ اللَّهِ بْنِ عَمْرٍو رضي الله عنهما قَالَ: رَأَيْتُ النَّبيَّ صلى الله عليه وسلم يَعْقِدُ التَّسْبِيحَ بِيَمِينِهِ.
Reference : Hisn al-Muslim 266
