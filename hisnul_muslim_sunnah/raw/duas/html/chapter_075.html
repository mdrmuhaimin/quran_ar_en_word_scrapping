Chapter: When insulted while fasting
(75)
ما يقول الصائم إذا سابه أحد
Hisn al-Muslim 186
Innī ṣā'im, innī ṣā'im. I am fasting. I am fasting. Reference: Al-Bukhari, cf. Al-Asqalani, Fathul-BAri 4/103, Muslim 2/806.
إِنِّي صَائِمٌ، إِنِّي صَائِمٌ
Reference : Hisn al-Muslim 186
